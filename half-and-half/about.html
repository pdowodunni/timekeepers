Our history

Built on experience

Timekeepers International Limited was registered in Nigeria in 2009. Founded on many years of experience, it was set up to import watches, jewellery and accessories from some of the world's leading brands, for distribution and retail in Nigeria and across the West African sub-region.

Today we are proud to manage a range of international, high-profile brands in both traditional and fashion watches.

Our business

Brand managers, not just a shop

We represent some of the world's best-known brands and manage them in the territories we cover. As brand managers, we are responsible for each brand's image and reputation here. We do this by striving to:

  1. 1Position every brand so it enjoys the same public perception here as in its home country and other leading markets.
  2. 2Give our customers the same experience, from purchase to after-sales service, as if they had bought from the brand in its home country.
